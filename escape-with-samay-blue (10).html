Referral programme

What is the timeline of this programme?

To be announced soon.

How does the referral programme work?

Share your personal link or code with a friend. They get 12% off their first order. Once their order is delivered, you each get 1,000 points towards a Samay Raina Live ticket.

Do I need to have shopped with Escape Plan to refer someone?

No. Anyone aged 18 or over in India can sign up as a referrer.

Where do I find my referral link?

Log in to the Escape Plan portal and open the Referral tab. You can copy your link or code, or share it directly on WhatsApp.

Who can use my link?

Friends who have never ordered on myescplan.com before. It works on HRX, Snitch and Rare Rabbit products on myescplan.com.

Can my friend use the 12% discount during a sale?

Yes. The referral discount works on top of sitewide offers. It cannot be combined with other coupon or creator codes.

When do we get the referral points?

As soon as your friend's order is delivered, 1,000 points are added to each of your accounts.

What if my friend cancels or returns the order?

Cancelled orders and parcels refused at delivery never earn points. If your friend returns the order, the referral points are removed from both your accounts.

My friend forgot to use my link. Can I still get the reward?

No. The link or code has to be used when the order is placed.

How many friends can I refer?

As many as you like. Every friend whose first order is delivered earns you 1,000 points.

Can I use my own link or a second account?

No. Self-referrals and multiple accounts are not allowed, and any rewards earned this way will be cancelled.

EscapeWithSamay points and tickets

What is EscapeWithSamay?

It's a campaign run by Esc Plan in collaboration with Samay Raina.

How do I earn points?

Three ways: shop HRX, Snitch or Rare Rabbit on myescplan.com, refer friends, and get a one-time credit for your past orders.

How many points do I get per order?

HRX and Snitch earn 1 point per ₹1. Rare Rabbit earns 2 points per ₹1. Points are based on what you pay after discounts.

Do my past orders count?

Yes. When you join, your earlier delivered orders on myescplan.com are credited once at 1 point per ₹10.

How many points do I get for referring a friend?

You get 1,000 points. Your friend gets 1,000 points plus 12% off their first order. Points are added to both accounts once their order is delivered.

When can I use my points?

Points are added once an order is delivered, and you can use them straight away.

How do I get my ticket?

Once you have 25,000 points, go to the Escape Plan portal and choose your show*. Before you confirm, we'll show you any orders still within the 30-day return window. Completing the redemption closes the return window for those orders. Each person can redeem up to 2 tickets, at 25,000 points each.

Can I choose my city?

You can choose from the shows available when you redeem. Cities depend on availability, so we cannot guarantee a particular one.

Can I give my ticket to someone else?

If you redeem 2 tickets, you can give the second one to a friend or family member. Tickets can't be resold.

Do my points expire?

Yes. Unused points expire when the campaign ends (date to be announced). Points have no cash value and cannot be transferred.

What happens to my points if I return an order?

If you return an order within the 30-day window, the points from it are removed. If it was a referral order, both you and your friend lose the referral points. Orders listed when you redeem a ticket can no longer be returned, though damaged or defective items can still be replaced.

Do kiosk or marketplace purchases count?

No. Only orders placed on myescplan.com earn points.

My points look wrong. Who do I contact?

Write to care@enchantebrands.com or call +91 97405 08909.

*Subject to availability.
