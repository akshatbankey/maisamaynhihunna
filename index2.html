Terms & conditions

The rules of the programme in plain words. Last updated 24 Sep 2026.

  1. Who runs it and who can joinRun by Enchante Brands Private Limited ("Escape Plan"). Open to customers who buy HRX, Snitch or Rare Rabbit on myescplan.com. One order is needed to register.
  2. How long it runsThe programme runs for 6 months, from 1 Sep 2026 to 28 Feb 2027. Points are valid only during this period and expire when it ends.
  3. When points are creditedPoints are posted to your wallet only after an order is delivered. If an order is cancelled, the points earned on it are reversed.
  4. Earning on your own ordersEvery delivered HRX or Snitch order earns a flat 750 points. Every delivered Rare Rabbit order earns a flat 1,500 points, whatever the order value.
  5. ReferralsA friend who orders through your referral link gets 12% off that order and 500 points. You get 500 points. Referrals count only for new customers who haven't ordered on myescplan.com before. Referral points are capped at 500 per referral for each person, and are credited only after the friend's order is delivered.
  6. Past ordersExisting customers get a one-time credit of 1 point per ₹10 of past D2C purchases, up to 5,000 points, added to their wallet at registration.
  7. Redeeming your ticket5,000 points redeem one ticket to Samay Raina Live. Each user can redeem one ticket during the programme. The show is at Good Shepherd Auditorium, Bengaluru, on Thu, 24 Sep 2026 at 7:00 PM, with general admission seating, subject to availability.
  8. Refunds on orders paid with creditsAny order, single or multiple, placed using programme credits cannot be refunded. It can still be replaced under Escape Plan's replacement policy.
  9. Points have no cash valuePoints cannot be exchanged for cash or transferred to another person.
  10. Changes and misuseEscape Plan may change or end the programme and may cancel points gained through misuse or fraud. Snitch, HRX and Rare Rabbit are trademarks of their respective owners.

Questions, answered

Still stuck? Write to us and we'll sort it out.

How do I join the programme?

Place any order for HRX, Snitch or Rare Rabbit on myescplan.com. Your first order registers you automatically.

When do my points show up?

Points stay pending until your order is delivered. Once it's delivered, they're added to your wallet.

What happens to my points if I cancel an order?

Any points earned on a cancelled order are reversed.

Do my old orders count?

Yes. When you register, your past D2C orders are credited once at ₹10 = 1 point, up to 5,000 points.

How does referring a friend work?

Share your referral link with someone who hasn't shopped on myescplan.com before. They get 12% off their first order, and once it's delivered you both get 500 points.

How many points does each order earn?

Each delivered HRX or Snitch order earns 750 points and each Rare Rabbit order earns 1,500 points, and every new order earns again.

Can I get more than one ticket?

No. Each user can redeem one Samay Raina Live ticket during the programme.

Where and when is the show?

Samay Raina Live is at Good Shepherd Auditorium, Bengaluru, on Thu, 24 Sep 2026. The show starts at 7:00 PM and seating is general admission.

Do my points expire?

Yes. All points expire when the 6-month programme ends on 28 Feb 2027.

Can I get a refund on an order I paid for with points?

Orders paid with programme credits can't be refunded, but you can still get a replacement.
